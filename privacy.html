Privacy policy

Aircrew Mission Log (Mission Log) keeps everything you enter on your iPad. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you log is sent to the developer or to anyone else unless you choose to share it.

Effective 23 November 2026. Applies to the iPad app and to the web version at missionlog.io.

What stays on your iPad

  • Missions, sorties, briefs, checkpoints, observations, notes, sketches, signatures, flight tracks and photos are stored only in the app's storage on your iPad.
  • Deleting the app deletes them. Back up with Export backup in the menu if you want to keep them.
  • Nothing is synced to iCloud or to any server by Mission Log.

Permissions and why

  • Location: shows your position, accuracy and CAP grid; stamps checkpoints and observations; and records the flight track from Engine Start to Shut Down, also while another app such as ForeFlight is in front. The track stays on the iPad.
  • Camera and photos: photos you attach to observations, and printed pages you scan into your notes. Scanned text is read on the iPad.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: only while you use Dictate in Notes, to turn speech into text. Mission Log asks for recognition on the iPad itself; where the iPad cannot do that, Apple's speech recognition may process the audio under Apple's privacy policy. Audio is not stored.
  • Notifications: reminders when an OPS check is due.
  • Live Activity: the sortie's phase and next OPS check on the Lock Screen.

You can change any of these in the iPad's Settings at any time.

What goes over the network

To show maps, Mission Log downloads chart tiles and aviation data for the area on screen, as any map does. These requests carry no personal information, but like every web request they reveal your IP address and the map area to the provider:

  • FAA aeronautical charts and the FAA Aeronautical Data Delivery Service (airspace, airports, navaids, waypoints)
  • USGS The National Map (topographic map)
  • Esri (shaded relief terrain)

Saved offline maps are kept on the iPad. The web version is served from GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.

When you share

PDFs, backups, GPX tracks and CAPF 104 forms leave the iPad only when you share them yourself, through the iPad's share sheet, to the person or app you choose. The feedback form is a Google Form you open on purpose; what you write there is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Children, changes and contact

Mission Log is made for Civil Air Patrol aircrew and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions go through the feedback form.
